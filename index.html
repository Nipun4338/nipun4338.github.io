Nipun Paul

Senior Software Engineer at Enosis Solutions · B.Sc. in CSE, AUST

I build reliable, data-driven software. For the past 3+ years I have designed and shipped full-stack enterprise systems (project management, payment approval and workspace reservation platforms) with TypeScript, NestJS, React and PostgreSQL.

My research goal is to make machine learning reliable where data is scarce: models that learn well from limited or simulated data, are tested honestly against simpler baselines, and turn complex scientific data into insights people can act on. This grew out of my IEEE paper on Bengali–English neural machine translation, and now drives my independent research in photonic sensor modelling and eDNA-based biodiversity monitoring.
